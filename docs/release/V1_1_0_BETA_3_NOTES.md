# ASTRA v1.1.0-beta.3 — clearer feedback, visible motion, included sources

**Candidate notes — not yet published.** Early beta for Windows x64. You check
the facts and apply on the employer's website yourself.

## What is new

- Setup and search-focus steps show persistent success and error messages, say
  why Continue is unavailable and never claim a save that failed. If a save
  worked but ASTRA could not refresh, press Refresh; nothing is saved twice.
- Panels, pages, accordions and Progress periods animate what actually changes.
  Settings → Appearance offers **Match system** (default), **Reduce motion** and
  **Full motion**. Windows animation settings are respected unless you choose Full.
- **Sources & websites** lists 77 official destinations: government, hospitals,
  companies, job portals and recruiters. 74 are links you open yourself and 3 are
  feeds ASTRA can scan (Cloudflare and Netcracker on Greenhouse, Lean Technologies
  on Ashby). Check dates are shown. 52 routes or feeds were reachable when checked;
  25 need a browser check or were inconclusive. This is not a claim that all 77 work,
  and a reachable page does not mean open or suitable jobs. You can add your own
  website; it is labelled unverified.
- New workspaces turn the three starter feeds on. Existing workspaces receive them
  paused; your sources and choices are kept. Opening ASTRA never scans.
- Optional Gmail explains plainly that advanced setup is needed in this beta.

## Limits

Scans stay manual and show a preview first. Public boards return global jobs, and
ASTRA applies your UAE and job-title settings, so results can be few. Matching
quality is not validated across majors. No public one-click Gmail sign-in, no
LinkedIn scraping, no scheduled scans. Rule-based matching is the default; AI
and Gmail are optional.
