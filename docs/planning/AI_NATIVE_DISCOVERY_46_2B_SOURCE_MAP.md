## What this does not claim

- **Role examples are not coverage.** They are a few titles, read on the evidence date, to show that a board carries UAE IT work. They are not counts of open jobs.
- **Readable is not supported.** Oracle, SuccessFactors, Avature, iCIMS, Phenom, Harbour, WhiteCarrot and custom boards have no ASTRA adapter. Any new reader needs a reviewed access basis and its own change.
- **Unresolved rows stay unresolved** until a person or a later permitted read confirms the official job board. An unverified lead is never an official link.
- **Nothing here is a scan result.** A controlled live scan is a separate, Owner-confirmed step; see [the controlled scan protocol](AI_NATIVE_DISCOVERY_46_2B_CONTROLLED_SCAN.md).
