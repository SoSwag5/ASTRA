## Stop-loop rule for every patch

After two focused repair attempts without useful progress, record the evidence,
owner, limitation and next experiment. Freeze a nonessential feature or remove it
from that patch's promise; continue the other bounded work. Do not bypass mandatory
security checks, silently lose data or label an unverified control PASS. Security,
privacy, artifact identity and broken core workflows require correction or a
policy-permitted explicit decision, not a cosmetic deferral.
